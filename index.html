GOOD TO KNOW

The essentials.

Open, flexible, and supported.

Who can join?

LEARN and BUILD are open to all JCSV III residents. No coding experience is needed. Join BUILD alone or with friends; attending LEARN is optional.

What goes in a BUILD proposal?

Describe your idea, what you hope to make, the AI tools you plan to use, and any subscription costs. Submit one proposal per project. The deadline is 11 October at 23:59 HKT.

What support and funding are available?

BUILD participants can access technical and design support. Selected eligible teams may also receive AI subscription reimbursement based on idea quality and feasibility.

A funded team must be led by an SHC resident who attends SHARE. Keep receipts for eligible subscriptions purchased between LEARN and SHARE; one reimbursement is available per individual or group.

Does my project need to be finished?

No. Finished projects, early prototypes, and lessons learned are all welcome at SHARE. Funded teams should discuss unfinished work with the programme team in advance.
