What to expect

  1. Team matching

    Join a team or form one with compatible builders. Matching needs mutual agreement and is not guaranteed.

  2. Co-working sessions

    Optional sessions to build together, ask questions and work through problems.

  3. WhatsApp community

    Share progress, meet fellow builders and receive programme updates.

  4. Demo Day

    Show a working prototype and what you learned. It does not need to be polished.

Three ways to focus your project

Choose by who you help and the problem you solve. Categories can overlap. No AI API is needed for these examples; AI can help build the code.

  • Everyday life

    Make everyday tasks easier for an individual or residents.

    Example: A pantry tracker with saved recipe matches and expiry reminders.

  • Team working

    Help a group coordinate tasks, people or shared information.

    Example: An event planner with role claiming, theme voting and a shared checklist.

  • Academics

    Support studying, learning or academic organisation.

    Example: Revision bingo with study challenges that reveal a progress picture.

Aim for functioning code you can demonstrate. Academic tools should support learning and protect academic integrity.

How AI subscription subsidy is selected

These criteria assess requests for AI subscription support. Funding selection does not affect participation in BUILD or access to general programme support.

  • Ethics

    Avoid harm, exclusion, cheating, surveillance and unnecessary private-data collection.

  • Practicality

    Solve a real problem for specific users who have a reason to use the solution again.

  • Feasibility

    Show how a small team can demonstrate a focused first version with running code in about one month.

  • AI usage and understanding

    Explain how AI helps, what people verify and what AI will not decide alone.

  • Originality

    Address a specific need rather than copying an existing product with a new name or appearance.

To be considered

Complete optional Sections 3–4 of the proposal. At least one team member must attend Demo Day in person.

Consideration does not guarantee support. Reimbursement requires valid receipts for eligible AI subscription expenses.

01 / EXPLORE

LEARN

Who can join LEARN?

Held on 7 October 2026, LEARN welcomed all JCSV III residents, with no coding experience required. BUILD applications remain open.

Do I need to attend LEARN before joining BUILD?

No. LEARN is a standalone workshop, and you may apply for BUILD whether or not you attended it.

Can I still join the programme?

Yes. BUILD applications are open, and everyone is welcome to apply, whether or not they attended LEARN.

02 / EXPERIMENT

BUILD

What is BUILD, and how much time will it require?

BUILD is a supported AI building fellowship. Develop a project at your own pace, with optional co-working activities and peer or technical support when you need it. There is no compulsory weekly schedule.

How can I apply?

Apply with a proposal if you already have a project idea, individually or as a team. Apply as an individual if you would like help finding people to build with.

Do I need an idea or a team already?

No. Individual applicants may be introduced to an existing proposal team or grouped with other compatible applicants to form a new team. Matching depends on mutual interest and is not guaranteed.

What can we build?

Any app or coded project is welcome, provided it produces functioning code that can be demonstrated. For example, a webpage connected to a functioning database would qualify.

What should the proposal contain?

Use the downloadable Markdown template. Sections 1–2 explain the problem, intended users, proposed solution and first working version, within 500 words combined. Sections 3–4 are optional and required only for subsidy consideration, with a separate 500-word limit.

What support will participants receive?

Participants can access co-working sessions, a community of fellow builders, and peer or technical guidance. More activities will begin in late October.

How does the AI subscription subsidy work?

Eligible projects may tentatively receive 100% reimbursement of approved AI subscription expenses, capped at HK$150 per approved individual or team.

To be considered, complete the optional subsidy sections of the proposal. At least one team member must attend Demo Day in person. Subsidy decisions are expected by 19 October. Support is not guaranteed, and valid receipts will be required.

What happens after I apply?

Applications close on 16 October at 23:59 HKT. Application and matching updates, together with subsidy decisions, are expected by 19 October. Co-working sessions and other BUILD activities will begin in late October.

03 / CONNECT

SHARE

What is Demo Day?

Demo Day is an informal evening where participants demonstrate what they built, exchange lessons and meet other residents interested in AI projects. It is expected to last around two hours and take place in mid-November. The exact date and venue will be announced later.

Does the project need to be completely finished?

No. The project does not need to be polished or commercially ready. Participants should aim to show functioning code and explain what they built, tested or learned.

Can I attend without presenting?

Yes. SHARE will offer separate registration for presenters and audience members closer to the event.
