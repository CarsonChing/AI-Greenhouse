A FEW PRACTICAL DETAILS

Good to Know

Room to explore.
Support along the way.

Who can join?+

LEARN and BUILD are open to all JCSV III residents, from any background. No coding experience is needed. Join BUILD on your own or in a group of any size; attending LEARN is not a requirement.

What should my BUILD proposal include?+

Tell us your idea, what you hope to create, which AI tools you plan to use, and any subscription costs. Submit one proposal per project. AI-assisted proposals and multiple submissions are welcome.

The application form is coming soon. The current deadline is 11 October 2026 at 23:59 HKT.

What support is available?+

Submitting a BUILD proposal gives you access to technical and design support from our team and connections with other participants. It’s a self-directed activity, without scheduled supervision.

Community and support arrangements are coming soon.

How does AI subscription support work?+

Selected eligible teams can receive reimbursement for AI subscriptions. Funding is assessed on idea quality and feasibility. To qualify, a team must be led by an SHC resident who serves as its representative and personally attends SHARE.

Keep your receipts. Eligible subscriptions must be purchased on or after LEARN and before SHARE. Receipts are due on Demo Day, before your team’s demonstration. Funding amounts and further claim details will be announced here.

Only one reimbursement is allowed per individual or group. If a member belongs to multiple groups, only one of those groups may receive reimbursement.

What if my project isn’t finished?+

An unfinished prototype or lessons learned can still be shared at SHARE. For funded teams, discuss this with the programme management team in advance so it can satisfy the demonstration requirement. The SHC-resident representative must still attend in person.
