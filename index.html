Q&A

Questions, answered.

Find what you need at each stage of AI Greenhouse.

01 / EXPLORE

LEARN

Who can join LEARN?

LEARN is open to all JCSV III residents. No coding experience is required.

Do I need to attend LEARN before joining BUILD?

No. LEARN is a standalone workshop, and you may apply for BUILD whether or not you attend it.

Should I bring anything?

Bringing a laptop is recommended so you can follow the demonstrations and try things yourself.

02 / EXPERIMENT

BUILD

What is BUILD, and how much time will it require?

BUILD is a supported AI building fellowship. Develop a project at your own pace, with optional co-working activities and peer or technical support when you need it. There is no compulsory weekly schedule.

How can I apply?

Apply with a proposal if you already have a project idea, individually or as a team. Apply as an individual if you would like help finding people to build with.

Do I need an idea or a team already?

No. Individual applicants may be introduced to an existing proposal team or grouped with other compatible applicants to form a new team. Matching depends on mutual interest and is not guaranteed.

What can we build?

Any app or coded project is welcome, provided it produces functioning code that can be demonstrated. For example, a webpage connected to a functioning database would qualify.

What should the proposal contain?

Use the downloadable Markdown template. Sections 1–2 explain the problem, intended users, proposed solution and first working version, within 500 words combined. Sections 3–4 are optional and required only for subsidy consideration, with a separate 500-word limit.

What support will participants receive?

Participants can access co-working sessions, a community of fellow builders, and peer or technical guidance. More activities will begin in late October.

How does the AI subscription subsidy work?

Eligible projects may tentatively receive 100% reimbursement of approved AI subscription expenses, capped at HK$150 per approved individual or team.

To be considered, complete the optional subsidy sections of the proposal. The team representative must be a Shun Hing College resident and be able to attend Demo Day in person. Subsidy decisions are expected by 16 October. Support is not guaranteed, and valid receipts will be required.

What happens after I apply?

Applications close on 13 October at 23:59 HKT. Application and matching updates, together with subsidy decisions, are expected by 16 October. Co-working sessions and other BUILD activities will begin in late October.

03 / CONNECT

SHARE

What is Demo Day?

Demo Day is an informal evening where participants demonstrate what they built, exchange lessons and meet other residents interested in AI projects. It is expected to last around two hours and take place in mid-November. The exact date and venue will be announced later.

Does the project need to be completely finished?

No. The project does not need to be polished or commercially ready. Participants should aim to show functioning code and explain what they built, tested or learned.

Can I attend without presenting?

Yes. SHARE will offer separate registration for presenters and audience members closer to the event.
